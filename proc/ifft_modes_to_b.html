public subroutine ifft_modes_to_B(m, n, B_mn, n_fft, fft_B)

Evaluate B(theta, phi) = sum_k B_mn(k)*cos(m(k)*theta - nfp*n(k)*phi) on the equidistant angle grid by a single 2D inverse FFT.

On the grid theta_j = 2*pi*(j-1)/n_fft, phi_l = 2*pi/nfp*(l-1)/n_fft the phase becomes 2*pi*(m*(j-1)/n_fft - n*(l-1)/n_fft) — nfp cancels and every phase is a root of unity. Writing the cosine as (exp(i*phase) + exp(-i*phase))/2 and scattering B_mn/2 onto both (m, -n) and (-m, n) therefore turns the mode sum into an unnormalised 2D inverse DFT of a Hermitian spectrum, whose transform is real.
